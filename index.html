About Us

A small club with a big love for stamps

We meet twice a month in Playa del Rey. No dues, no dress code, no intimidating experts who'll make you feel out of place.

Whether you've got a shoebox of inherited stamps with no idea what you've got, or you've been collecting for decades with a very specific want list — you're welcome here.

What you'll find: good conversation, genuine enthusiasm, and an auction that makes it way too easy to come home with more stamps than you planned to.

  • Open to all skill levels — beginners to experts
  • About 6 active members
  • Free to attend, no membership required

Why You'll Love It

What happens at every meeting

You could spend the whole evening browsing, bidding, or trading — or all three. Here's what's on offer.

01

60-Lot Dutch Auction

Every single meeting. Sixty lots of stamps up for bidding, often going for a fraction of catalog value. The deals are genuine.

02

US & Worldwide Premium Sets

Sets from around the globe — US and worldwide — available well below face value. These aren't the common stuff.

03

US Mint at 75¢ or Less

US Mint stamps priced at 75 cents on the dollar or less. If you use stamps for postage, this alone is worth showing up for.

04

Approval Albums

Browse our albums and buy only what you want. No pressure, no minimums. Pick exactly what fits your collection.

05

Member Trading

Got duplicates? Need something specific? Members trade directly with each other. The best deals sometimes happen over a handshake.

06

We Buy Collections

Have a collection you're ready to sell? We buy collections for future auctions. Talk to us — we're genuinely interested.
